# GP Farm – Android app

## [⬇ Download GP-Farm.apk](https://github.com/DarrenGwende/gp-farm-app/releases/latest/download/GP-Farm.apk)

This link always gives the newest version.

### How to install
1. Open the link above on your Android phone (Android 7.0 or newer).
2. Tap the downloaded **GP-Farm.apk**.
3. If asked, allow installing apps from this source, then tap **Install**.
4. Open **GP Farm** and sign in with your GP Farm account.

To update, download and install again; your data stays in your account.
